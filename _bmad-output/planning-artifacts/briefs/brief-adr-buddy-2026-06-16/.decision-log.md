# Decision Log

## 2026-06-16

- Create a collaborative Planning Poker application for estimating user stories.
- Support two access types: Administrator / Moderator and Participant.
- Moderator creates or manages the estimation session.
- Moderator defines the current user story, including story number or identifier and brief description.
- Only the moderator can start the estimation round.
- Multiple participants can connect to the same estimation session.
- Participants can see estimation cards and choose one card as their estimate.
- Initial estimation configurations include T-shirt sizes and Fibonacci sequence values.
- Future direction may include card themes or custom images to make cards more attractive and fun.
- The product is intended as an internal tool, so the brief should prioritize team workflow fit, implementation scope, and low-friction adoption over public launch positioning or monetization.
- Initial audience is one internal team, not multiple squads or company-wide use.
- Participants should join by room code.
- Votes should stay hidden until the moderator reveals them.
- The moderator should be able to record the final estimate for the current story.
- Revealed cards should be grouped or ordered by number of votes to make consensus and outliers easy to read.
- Version 1 should not include Jira, Azure DevOps, or other backlog integrations.
- The moderator can also vote in the estimation round.
- The session should preserve a list of estimated stories.
