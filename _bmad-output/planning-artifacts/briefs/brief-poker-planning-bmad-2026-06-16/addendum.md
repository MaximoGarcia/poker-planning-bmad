# Addendum

## Discovery Extracts

### Planning Poker Method

- Planning Poker is used to estimate user stories collaboratively while reducing anchoring bias by keeping estimates hidden until participants reveal together.
- A typical flow includes presenting the story, letting estimators choose cards privately, revealing simultaneously, discussing outliers, and repeating until the team reaches consensus.
- Common decks include Fibonacci-style story points, T-shirt sizes, and special cards such as question/pass/break-style cards.

### Light Landscape Notes

- Simple tools emphasize instant room creation, link or room-code sharing, no mandatory signup, visible participant presence, hidden voting, reveal/reset controls, and mobile browser support.
- More advanced tools add backlog integrations, automatic sync back to Jira/GitHub/GitLab, built-in discussion, revoting, final estimate selection, meeting summaries, custom scales, multi-dimensional formats, organization-shared estimation approaches, and optional engagement features.
- For this product brief, the first-version opportunity appears to be a focused, low-friction collaborative estimation flow rather than competing immediately on backlog integrations or full agile meeting management.
