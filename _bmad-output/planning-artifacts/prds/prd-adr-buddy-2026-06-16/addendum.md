# Addendum

## Source Notes Preserved From Product Brief

- Planning Poker reduces anchoring bias by keeping estimates hidden until participants reveal together.
- A typical flow presents a Story, lets estimators choose Cards privately, reveals simultaneously, discusses outliers, and repeats until the team reaches consensus.
- Common Decks include Fibonacci-style story points, T-shirt sizes, and special cards such as question, pass, or break-style cards.
- Comparable lightweight tools emphasize instant room creation, link or Room Code sharing, no mandatory signup, participant presence, hidden voting, reveal/reset controls, and mobile browser support.
- More advanced tools add backlog integrations, automatic sync to planning systems, built-in discussion, revoting, final estimate selection, meeting summaries, custom scales, organization-shared estimation approaches, and engagement features.
